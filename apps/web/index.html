4 Spend a budget: open or buy?

A opens n boosters and sells every card worth selling. B spends the same budget on the most expensive single cards that fit, then sells them back. Π is the net profit in USD (negative = loss).

5 Chase one card: open until it appears, or buy it?

A opens boosters until the target card appears and sells the other cards; after nmax boosters without it, A gives up and buys the card. Π = price of the card − net cost of opening, so Π > 0 means opening was cheaper. B buys the card directly (Π = 0). Targets are named by rarity; their price is not shown.

6 Hypotheses, limits and data

Limits

  • Prices are a provider's market estimate on one date, not observed sales.
  • Shipping costs and liquidity haircuts are assumptions, stated in step 2.
  • Pull rates come from community openings; their uncertainty is propagated (95 % intervals).
  • Three English-language sets, booster packs only, no grading. Results do not generalise to other sets, dates or markets.
  • This is a research result, not financial advice.
